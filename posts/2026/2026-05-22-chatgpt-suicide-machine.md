> LLM chatbots like ChatGPT have been responsible for [dozens of confirmed suicides, murders and serious mental health crises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deaths_linked_to_chatbots), particularly involving children and young people. Any product that is this dangerous to young people would normally be nowhere near a classroom. And would likely be banned outright. But thanks to the cult-like AI-hype that has consumed the brains of our government and corporate leaders, they only want more integration of these products into education, not less.

Leider hervorragendes, satirisches Werbebanner von [Darren Cullen](https://www.spellingmistakescostlives.com/darren-cullen), das die Problematik ziemlich gut zusammenfasst.
