> Reading Maps came out of a nagging thought that every story has happened somewhere. Odysseus crossed a real sea. The Pequod sailed real water. Phileas Fogg's eighty days ran on real railways and real steamer lines and that you could follow him in a way.

Schönes Projekt von [Aires Loutsaris](https://airesloutsaris.com/), das fiktionale Reisen und Abenteuer auf die echten Orte legt. Hier etwa »[Moby Dick](https://readingmaps.com/maps/moby-dick)«, »[Fargo](https://readingmaps.com/maps/fargo)«, »[Forrest Gump](https://readingmaps.com/maps/forrest-gump)« oder – aus natürlich aktuellem Hype-Anlass – »[The Odyssee](https://readingmaps.com/maps/the-odyssey)«. (via [Thomas](https://gigold.me/links/literarische-reisen-visualisiert))
