> On 16 September 1997, Steve Jobs [became interim CEO](https://www.wired.com/2010/09/0916jobs-out-in-apple/) of Apple. [5,090 days later](https://www.wolframalpha.com/input?i=number+of+days+between+16+september+1997+and+24+august+2011), [he handed the reins](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2011/08/24Steve-Jobs-Resigns-as-CEO-of-Apple/) to Tim Cook, weeks before he died.
>
> 5,090 days after 24 August 2011 [is today](https://www.wolframalpha.com/input?i=5090+days+after+24+august+2011). The Cook era is now as long as the Jobs renaissance era.

Tim Cook ist seit gestern genau so lange (bzw. heute länger) im Amt als Steve Jobs nach seiner Rückkehr zu Apple. 🤯
